It is always difficult, as a photographer, to express photos through words and to describe why I decided to take and post this or that photo. It’s even more difficult when the main thing I wanted to convey is the feeling of calmness and tranquility Finland instills when you travel across the country.

> ![Photo of a lake in Finland in autumn](/images/finland-1.jpg "Autumn lake, Finland")

This is one of the many places on the western side of Näsijärvi, to the north of Tampere. The main photo isn’t from Näsijärvi itself, however, but rather from one of the small lakes next to it, at a totally random spot where I decided to stop while heading north to the small towns of Ruovesi and Keuruu. A small lake, a short river flowing into it, a sauna and nothing around except birds, sounds of wind and the rustling of leaves. 

> ![View of a small island in a lake in Finland](/images/finland-6.jpg "Autumn, Finland")

Not sure if I didn’t accidentally stumble upon someone’s private property here. This is one of the things I’m never sure about in Finland, since there are few obvious signs telling “private property”, unlike in the US or in many European countries. Even easier in where I come from, where private property would usually have a fence (or a literal 2 m tall wall) around it, regardless of size. Hope nobody’s peace was disturbed during this little stop.
