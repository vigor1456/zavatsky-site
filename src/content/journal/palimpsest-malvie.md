Palimpsest is a portrait series about dissolution in the search for self. The model is wrapped in hand-painted fabric whose marks continue into the set around her, so she seems both born from her surroundings and slowly consumed by them. The gestural strokes read like writing that can’t be deciphered, a reality authored by someone else.

![Fine art photograph of a girl, covered in a white bedsheet painted with black paint](/images/Malvie_LogoBig_Palimpsest_Image02.jpg)

In Palimpsest, Eliška Macharová is wrapped in white fabric covered with black gestural brushstrokes, and the same marks run across the floor and walls around her, so that she seems to grow out of the set and disappear back into it.

![Fine art photograph of a girl, covered in a white bedsheet painted with black paint, with only eyes visible](/images/Malvie_LogoBig_Palimpsest_Image03.jpg)

We move from frames where she almost vanishes, lying in the folds or veiled to the eyes, to direct portraits lit against a band of red, the painted cloth drawn up like a hood.

![Fine art photograph of a girl, covered in a white bedsheet painted with black paint, with bright red light creeping in](/images/Malvie_LogoBig_Palimpsest_Image06.jpg)
