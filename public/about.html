The person behind both worlds

Learning to watch.

scroll

For most of my life, I’ve been learning to watch.

As a forensic pathologist, I looked at what remains after a story.
As a data analyst, I looked for what’s hidden in the noise.
As a diplomat, I looked at what people reveal when they think no one is watching.
As an inmate in an illegal prison, I saw how people break under a weight they never chose.

It was always the same, but now I finally have a camera in my hands.

In my personal projects, I carefully craft every scene, every detail, every emotion, and every expression. Every light in my photographs is intentional. I strive to make each shot feel like a scene from a movie that hasn’t been made yet.

One eye, two bodies of work.

Zavatsky for the people, Ravique for the places.
